      Packing is guillotine best-area fit with a shorter-leftover split. Panels go largest longer-side first, into the free rectangle whose leftover area is smallest. Each placement is one through-cut of that rectangle: if the leftover is narrower than it is short, the cut runs the full height; otherwise it runs the full width. The panel may be turned 90° unless face direction is locked. Stock sheets are not turned. This is not an exact optimum. Saw kerf (kerf_mm) defaults to 3 mm and is taken out of each through-cut. A leftover thinner than the kerf is not used. Set kerf to 0 for score-and-snap.
